Bowling score calculator — informal requirements

We need a small service that scores a single game of ten-pin bowling for one
player. A game has 10 frames. In each frame the player rolls up to two balls
to knock down 10 pins. The score should be available through a simple HTTP
endpoint that takes the list of rolls and returns the total score.

Feature 1 — open frames: if the player never knocks down all 10 pins in a
frame, the game score is just the total number of pins knocked down. A full
gutter game scores 0. A game of all single-pin rolls scores 20.

Feature 2 — spares: knocking down all 10 pins with the two rolls of a frame
is a spare; that frame scores 10 plus the number of pins knocked down by the
next roll.

Feature 3 — strikes: knocking down all 10 pins with the first roll of a frame
is a strike; that frame scores 10 plus the pins knocked down by the next two
rolls. A perfect game (12 strikes) scores 300.

Feature 4 — the tenth frame: a spare in the tenth frame earns one bonus roll,
a strike in the tenth frame earns two; bonus rolls only count as bonus for
the tenth frame, they are not frames of their own.

Work the features in order, one Tran Forge cycle each.
